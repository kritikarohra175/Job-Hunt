# Red Flags and Hard Stops

Immediately reject or route to review when any of the following appears:

- Candidate must pay a fee to apply, interview, train, or obtain equipment.
- Candidate must purchase a product or service.
- Commission-only compensation where the role is presented as marketing.
- MLM/network marketing/business-opportunity language.
- Role is primarily telesales, field sales, insurance sales, collections, or telecalling.
- Employer identity is materially unclear or suspicious.
- JD is copied, contradictory, or materially different across sources.
- Unusually urgent request for money, identity documents, banking credentials, OTPs, or passwords.
- Request to download untrusted software or browser extensions unrelated to the application.
- CAPTCHA/anti-bot/identity controls that the agent cannot legitimately complete.
- Request to falsify experience, credentials, employment dates, or answers.
- Requirement to make a payment or sign a legal contract as part of the application.
- Application asks for sensitive information without a legitimate and appropriate context.

Never bypass a red flag. Record the reason.
